The thinking behind the work

A multidisciplinary path, with people at the centre.

From making interfaces to understanding the audiences and brands behind them.

My tech journey began this year with Product Design, where I discovered my passion for creating meaningful digital experiences. That curiosity quickly led me into Web Development, allowing me to transform ideas into responsive, user-friendly websites.

I later expanded into Digital Marketing, learning how to build brands, connect with audiences, and grow businesses through strategy and creativity. To put these skills into practice, I launched Eco-Closet, my personal brand, where I combine design, development, and marketing to build a real-world digital experience.

Today, I'm passionate about creating digital products and brands that blend creativity, technology, and strategy—and I'm excited to keep learning, building, and growing.
